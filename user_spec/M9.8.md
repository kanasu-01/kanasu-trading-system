# Kanasu M9.8 — Complete Functional Specification

## Our Goal

Kanasu shall provide a complete research-to-paper-trading workflow through three frontend pages:

Backtest → WFA → Paper Trading

The Backtest page shall use Start Backtest. On the WFA page, the user shall first check WFA eligibility, choose from eligible strategies, and then start WFA. On the Paper Trading page, the user shall first check Paper eligibility, choose from eligible strategies, and then start or continue Paper Trading.

Kanasu shall support multiple independently tested strategy configurations within a Study. Paper Trading shall use one continuous simulated account shared by the selected eligible strategies.

Kanasu shall preserve exact Study, strategy-configuration, saved-result, qualification-decision, and execution identities.

## 1. Backtest Frontend Page — Start Backtest and Save Results

1.1. The user opens the Backtest page.

1.2. The user selects a Study, the exact strategy configuration to test, and the required Backtest settings.

1.3. The user clicks Start Backtest.

1.4. Kanasu executes the selected Backtest independently, using its own configured starting capital; results and balances from another strategy test shall not carry over.

1.5. The Backtest result is displayed on the frontend.

1.6. Kanasu durably saves each completed Backtest result without overwriting earlier attempts.

1.7. The saved result remains linked to the exact tested configuration and conditions (including its instrument, period, data, parameters, and other recorded research inputs).

1.8. The user can return later and browse earlier saved Backtest results, including unsuccessful and outdated attempts, without rerunning them.

1.9. During Backtest, Kanasu calculates and saves the measurements needed for qualification. Completing Backtest does not itself mean that the result qualifies for WFA.

Expected outcome: A completed Backtest and its saved results are available for the next stage.

1.10. Each Backtest test or retry shall keep its own recorded outcome; one strategy’s profit or loss shall never change the starting capital of another independent historical evaluation.

## 2. Backtest Frontend Page — Browse Saved Results for WFA

2.1. The user opens the Backtest page and selects a Study.

2.2. Kanasu displays the Study’s saved Backtest results, including results that failed, are incomplete, or are no longer current.

2.3. Each saved Backtest result shall remain linked to its exact Study, strategy configuration, tested stock or universe, period, execution attempt, research evidence, and qualification decision.

2.4. Selecting strategies to execute in WFA takes place on the WFA page, after Check WFA Eligibility; it is not necessary to send a selection from the Backtest page.

2.5. The Backtest page permits historical result browsing. Qualification for a new WFA run is checked on the WFA page against the applicable current criteria.

2.6. Checking WFA eligibility shall load saved Backtest results for the selected Study and verify them under the current qualification policy. When the exact evidence, policy version, and decision authority remain unchanged, Kanasu shall reuse the existing valid qualification decision rather than append a replacement.

2.7. Kanasu shall preserve the recorded qualification decisions and any reasons for rejection, insufficient evidence, invalid evidence, or operational failure; repeated checks under unchanged conditions shall not create replacement decisions.

2.8. Only currently eligible saved Backtest results shall be offered for WFA selection, with each selectable entry linked to its exact result and qualification decision.

2.9. The original saved Backtest results remain available and unchanged.

2.10. Backtest results that do not qualify remain accessible in Backtest history but do not appear in the eligible-strategy checkbox list on the WFA page.

Expected outcome: Saved Backtest results are available for eligibility checking on the WFA page.

## 3. WFA Frontend Page — Start WFA and Save Results

3.1. The user opens the WFA page and can identify the selected Study.

3.2. The user clicks Check WFA Eligibility; Kanasu loads saved Backtest results for the selected Study and checks current Backtest qualification.

3.3. Kanasu shall display only currently eligible saved Backtest results with checkboxes. Each entry shall use the agreed strategy display name, with additional identifying details when names repeat. The user may select individual eligible results or Select All.

3.4. The user clicks Start WFA only after choosing eligible strategies. Check WFA Eligibility prepares the list; Start WFA begins the selected execution.

3.5. When Start WFA is clicked, Kanasu shall validate the submitted identities of every selected saved Backtest result and its supporting qualification decision against current evidence and settings. If any selected entry fails final admission, Kanasu shall block the entire submission and start no WFA execution for it. Kanasu shall identify the blocked entries and reasons; it shall not silently omit or substitute entries based on display names or Study selection.

3.6. Only the selected and verified eligible strategies undergo WFA, independently and sequentially, using their own historical starting capital and the existing fixed-candidate WFA methodology.

3.7. Kanasu displays the execution status and saved WFA results of each selected configuration; a technical failure is not a WFA economic FAIL.

3.8. As each selected strategy completes WFA, Kanasu durably saves the historical measurements, supporting evidence, completion order, and PASS/FAIL decision, linked to the exact saved Backtest result, strategy configuration, and supporting qualification decision.

3.9. The user can browse previously saved WFA results and historical PASS/FAIL outcomes, even when those results are no longer eligible for a new Paper session, without rerunning WFA.

3.10. Saved WFA results, including their historical verdicts, become available for later Paper eligibility checks and historical browsing.

3.11. The existing WFA engine shall determine and save the WFA PASS/FAIL verdict using its current six fixed criteria. Checking Paper eligibility shall verify this saved verdict, not recalculate it from dashboard values.

3.12. If WFA fails technically or is interrupted, Kanasu shall preserve valid, fully completed results for individual selected strategies and record their available status. Resuming the incomplete operation shall reuse those completed results and rerun unfinished or technically invalid strategy evaluations from the beginning, not resume partway through a calculation. A technical failure shall not become an economic FAIL.

Expected outcome: Only selected qualified strategies undergo WFA and generate saved results when they actually complete.

3.13. When Start WFA is clicked, Kanasu shall durably save the complete list of eligible saved Backtest results presented to the user and the exact subset selected, including their supporting qualification decisions and submission time. If any selected entry fails final admission, Kanasu shall retain the submitted lists, blocked status, affected entries, and reasons; no WFA execution shall start for that submission.

3.14. WFA execution shall begin only after every selected exact saved result passes final admission. Once actual WFA execution starts, Kanasu shall record its status and durably save each completed strategy’s measurements, evidence, and PASS/FAIL decision. A blocked submission shall not be recorded as a started or completed WFA execution.

3.15. Each Start WFA submission shall have a distinct request identity. Repeated delivery of the same request, accidental double-clicks, or lost responses shall reconcile to that request without duplicate execution. A retry shall reuse valid completed strategy results; a deliberately new WFA execution shall use a new request and execution identity, preserving earlier results.

3.16. Kanasu shall distinguish Start WFA for a deliberate new execution from Resume Incomplete WFA for recovery of an earlier operation. Both actions shall verify current eligibility; neither shall silently rerun Backtest or replace an unchanged qualification decision.

## 4. WFA Frontend Page — Browse Saved Results for Paper Trading

4.1. The user may browse historical WFA results on the WFA page for a selected Study.

4.2. Kanasu keeps all saved WFA results and their original PASS/FAIL decisions accessible, including failed or outdated historical runs.

4.3. Selection of strategies for qualified Paper Trading takes place on the Paper Trading page after Check Paper Eligibility.

4.4. The WFA page shall not require a Send to Paper or separate evaluation action to initiate qualified Paper Trading selection.

4.5. An original WFA PASS or FAIL remains a permanent historical verdict. A historical PASS alone does not guarantee that a new Paper session can start.

4.6. For the same exact saved Backtest result and supporting qualification decision, the most recently completed, technically valid WFA PASS or FAIL decision shall be authoritative. An incomplete, interrupted, or technically failed WFA evaluation shall not supersede that decision. Different exact saved Backtest results or supporting qualification decisions shall retain separate WFA decision authority. Paper eligibility shall require the current authoritative WFA PASS and intact current Backtest qualification chain. The Paper Trading page shall not independently recalculate the six WFA criteria or overwrite historical verdicts.

4.7. For historical WFA results that cannot currently progress, Kanasu shall show the recorded result, blocking reason, and the next possible action in the history or relevant status area, not in the eligible-only checkbox list.

4.8. Only exact saved WFA results supported by the current authoritative WFA PASS decision, intact current Backtest qualification authority, and required evidence shall be offered for Paper Trading selection. An older PASS superseded by a newer authoritative WFA decision shall remain historical and shall not be selectable.

4.9. The original saved WFA results and qualification history remain available and unchanged.

4.10. If the current Backtest qualification decision changes, an older WFA decision linked to different Backtest authority remains historical and cannot authorize a new qualified Paper session. Likewise, a WFA PASS superseded by a newer authoritative WFA decision cannot authorize a new session. Kanasu shall explain the blocking reason and identify when a new WFA run is required, without starting one automatically.

Expected outcome: Historical WFA results are available for eligibility checking on the Paper Trading page.

## 5. Paper Trading Frontend Page — Start Paper Trading and Save Results

5.1. The user opens the Paper Trading page.

5.2. The user selects a Study and clicks Check Paper Eligibility. Kanasu loads saved WFA results and verifies each candidate against the current authoritative WFA PASS decision, its exact current Backtest qualification, and required research evidence.

5.3. Kanasu shall display only saved WFA results supported by the current authoritative WFA PASS decision and valid current Backtest qualification, with checkboxes. The user may select one, several, or all eligible entries.

5.4. The user clicks Start / Continue Paper Trading after selecting one or more eligible WFA results. Check Paper Eligibility prepares the list but does not begin trading.

5.5. At actual Start / Continue admission, Kanasu shall recheck every exact selected saved WFA result against the current authoritative WFA PASS decision, its current Backtest qualification, and operational readiness, including executable evidence, provider and instrument binding, supported configuration, market-data preparation, account recovery, and runtime ownership. If multiple selected WFA results refer to the same exact strategy configuration, or if any selected entry or shared-account safety condition fails, Kanasu shall block the whole submitted selection and admit none of its entries. A blocked submission shall leave the previously accepted new-entry authorization set unchanged, subject to independently applicable eligibility and safety restrictions, and shall not interrupt necessary protective management of existing positions.

5.6. Paper Trading uses real market data and simulated orders and account execution; it must not place real-money trading orders.

5.7. Kanasu displays current Paper Trading status, shared-account information, strategy-specific activity, and saved session results.

5.8. The user may Stop Paper Trading. Stop ends the current trading activity and preserves the one shared simulated account; it shall not automatically liquidate existing positions or reset cash.

5.9. During Paper Trading, Kanasu shall durably save shared-account and trading state periodically and promptly after significant events or incidents. On normal Stop or completion it shall save the final available account state and session results; unexpected interruptions shall be recorded truthfully using the last reliably saved state.

5.10. The user can browse previous Paper Trading sessions, incidents, strategy-specific activity, and available saved account results.

5.11. Each selected strategy shall retain its own trading decisions and necessary protective state while using the shared Paper Trading account.

5.12. Kanasu shall allow multiple eligible selected strategies to trade simultaneously through one continuous shared simulated account, with coordinated cash, position, and risk management. Each exact strategy configuration shall have at most one active Paper Trading execution instance.

5.13. Each strategy’s trade activity and results shall remain separately identifiable, while the cash, positions, and account-level risk remain shared.

5.14. Qualified Paper Trading shall maintain one continuous simulated trading account for all strategies. Kanasu shall initialize that account once using its configured Paper Trading starting capital; subsequent Start / Continue operations shall reuse the existing account state without resetting or replenishing its capital. Cash, positions, trading history, and required strategy/protective state shall survive Stop/Start cycles and application restarts, including positions held over multiple trading days.

5.15. Each Paper Trading session shall have its own history, but all qualified sessions shall use the same continuous shared account. Each accepted Start / Continue selection shall define the complete set of strategy configurations authorized to open new positions, replacing the previous new-entry authorization set. A previously selected configuration selected again shall continue with its preserved account positions and applicable trading state, without creating a duplicate active instance. A new session or newly selected strategy shall not create a fresh independent account or silently transfer or discard positions.

5.16. After an unexpected interruption, Kanasu shall preserve the last durably saved state, mark the session as interrupted without fabricating a final snapshot or missed trades, and restore and verify the shared account before resuming. If this cannot be done reliably, unsafe trading shall remain blocked with an explanation.

5.17. Changing qualification or strategy configuration shall not erase shared-account positions or cause automatic liquidation. Ineligible strategies and previously authorized strategies omitted from the latest accepted selection shall not open new positions; while Paper Trading is running, existing positions shall continue to receive necessary protective management when safe, and any unresolved state shall be retained for controlled resolution.

5.18. Kanasu shall keep research-qualified Paper Trading separate from standalone operational testing, clearly label standalone sessions as non-qualified, and prevent their results from counting as research qualification.

Expected outcome: Selected, authorized strategies can start or safely continue qualified Paper Trading with continuously preserved shared-account state and recorded session outcomes.

5.19. All selected Paper Trading strategies shall use the same available simulated cash, existing positions, and account-level risk limits. Kanasu shall prevent different strategies from spending the same available cash twice.

5.20. When Start / Continue Paper Trading is clicked, Kanasu shall durably save the complete eligible WFA PASS list and the exact saved WFA results and decisions selected by the user, including submission time. If any selected entry or required operational safeguard fails admission, Kanasu shall block the entire submission, retain the submitted list, selected subset, affected entries, and reasons, and start no trading activity for that submission. It shall not record an active session or simulated trades that never started.

5.21. When several strategies trade through the shared account, Kanasu shall enforce account-wide limits and safely handle competing orders or position actions rather than permitting inconsistent cash or position state.

5.22. An unavailable provider, invalid instrument binding, unsupported timeframe, failed market-data preparation, unsafe shared-account recovery, or another active runtime owner shall block operational admission without changing a historical WFA PASS into FAIL.

5.23. Reliable continuation shall preserve the last processed market observation, pending trading actions, protective settings, and risk state needed to avoid duplicate or invented simulated trades after a restart.

## 6. Common Requirements for Research and Paper Trading

6.1. A Study is a user-recognizable research investigation grouping related work. In the frontend, a Strategy means one exact tested configuration of an algorithm. Different configurations do not share qualification or result identities merely because they use the same algorithm.

6.2. Every saved result and qualification decision shall retain the exact Study, configuration, stock or universe, tested period, data and settings, physical execution attempt, and immutable supporting evidence that produced it.

6.3. The user shall be able to browse historical results and separately understand current eligibility, blocking reason, and next action on the relevant page. These are types of information, not a requirement for separate screens.

6.4. Only a selected strategy that satisfies the currently required research and operational admission checks may start the next execution stage.

6.5. Previously saved results shall remain available even when a strategy does not qualify for the next stage.

6.6. A failed or unavailable evaluation or execution shall not be recorded as a successful result or as an economic evaluation FAIL unless the applicable criterion was actually evaluated and not satisfied.

6.7. Each saved Backtest result shall trace to its qualification decision; each WFA result shall trace to the Backtest decision that admitted it; each qualified Paper session shall trace to the saved WFA PASS and current authority that admitted it.

6.8. Checking eligibility or starting a later stage shall not silently rerun a completed earlier Backtest or WFA. When the exact evidence, applicable criteria version, and decision authority are unchanged, Kanasu shall reuse the existing valid qualification decision rather than create a replacement.

6.9. Historical PASS/FAIL verdicts remain saved as recorded. A historical PASS need not mean that the current research evidence and operational checks authorize a new Paper session.

6.10. Within a Study, one algorithm may have multiple parameter configurations. Each completed research run shall retain its own independently identifiable result and history.

6.11. Selecting one configuration or saved result shall not automatically select another configuration or another result that happens to share the same display name.

6.12. Displayed strategy names are for recognition; the exact saved result and decision identities shall govern which evidence Kanasu uses and which work Kanasu executes.

6.13. Users shall first select a Study and then choose eligible saved results within that Study. Kanasu shall not mix results from different Studies or substitute a different result with the same display name.

6.14. There are three execution pages: Backtest has Start Backtest; WFA has Check WFA Eligibility followed by Start WFA; Paper Trading has Check Paper Eligibility followed by Start / Continue Paper Trading. The two Check actions prepare eligibility and do not execute their stages.

6.15. Kanasu shall prevent duplicate execution of the same submitted request and reconcile lost responses against saved records. An incomplete WFA operation shall reuse valid, fully completed selected-strategy results and rerun only unfinished or technically invalid strategy evaluations from the beginning; a deliberately new execution shall have a distinct identity. Stale settings, unsafe admission, or conflicting Paper runtime ownership that cannot be safely coordinated shall block execution.

6.16. When progression is blocked, Kanasu shall explain the cause and next action. A technical or operational failure shall not be mislabelled as a failed economic evaluation.

6.17. Historical rejected, failed, invalid, insufficient, and outdated results remain browsable, but the WFA and Paper Trading checkbox lists shall display only currently eligible strategies.

6.18. Checkbox selections remain unexecuted user choices until Start WFA or Start / Continue Paper Trading is submitted. Kanasu shall then save the eligible and selected lists with the submitted request’s accepted or blocked status. If any selected entry fails final admission, the entire submitted selection shall be blocked with affected entries and reasons recorded; no subset shall execute silently. Kanasu shall create actual execution results only for work that started and completed as recorded.

6.19. Saved eligible lists, selected subsets, requests, execution attempts, results, and qualification decisions shall be traceable to the exact Study, strategy configuration, stock or universe, period, saved evidence, and decision authority. Earlier records shall not be overwritten.

6.20. The user-facing strategy display name shall follow StudyName_StrategyName_UniverseOrStock_Period, where StrategyName identifies the exact parameter configuration. If two saved results have the same display name, Kanasu shall show additional result details to distinguish them. Display names shall not replace exact internal result identities.

## 7. Central Research Settings

7.1. Kanasu shall provide one central Research Settings location showing the evaluation criteria applicable between research stages and whether each criterion is currently editable.

7.2. Each evaluation criterion shall identify its measurement, comparison operator, required value, and active or mandatory status. Research Settings shall distinguish economic research criteria from separate operational Paper Trading admission checks.

7.3. The current editable Backtest-to-WFA criterion is net-profitable completed-trade rate: profitable completed trades divided by all completed trades, including breakeven trades, multiplied by 100. A net-profitable trade has positive profit after applicable costs. Decisions shall compare exact unrounded counts with the configured threshold; a displayed rounded percentage shall never decide eligibility. Equality passes; zero completed trades means INSUFFICIENT.

7.4. Each criteria-version change shall preserve the previous criteria version and its associated saved evaluation decisions. A new version shall govern future eligibility checks without rewriting historical outcomes.

7.5. WFA shall calculate and save a permanent historical PASS/FAIL verdict using the six fixed criteria: consistency ≥ 60%; average out-of-sample account return > 0%; worst out-of-sample equity drawdown ≤ 20%; stitched out-of-sample return > 0%; stitched maximum drawdown ≤ 20%; account-return stability ≥ 0.20. Research Settings shall display these as read-only. Paper Trading shall verify the saved WFA verdict rather than recalculate it.

7.6. Where a criterion is authorized to be editable, Research Settings shall allow changing its value through a new saved criteria version; previous criteria versions and their evaluation history shall not be overwritten.

7.7. Previously saved Backtest, WFA, qualification, and Paper Trading evidence shall remain unchanged when settings are updated.

7.8. Check WFA Eligibility shall verify saved Backtest evidence and reuse any unchanged valid qualification decision. Check Paper Eligibility and Start / Continue Paper Trading shall verify the exact saved WFA result, its current authoritative WFA PASS decision, and its exact current upstream Backtest qualification; WFA verdicts shall not be recalculated in Paper Trading.

7.9. The editable Backtest qualification policy shall be global across Studies. Kanasu shall warn when a policy change affects qualifications associated with other Studies or earlier downstream results.

7.10. Changing Research Settings shall not automatically rerun Backtests, WFA, or Paper Trading.

7.11. Research Settings shall show the current qualification policy version and shall allow users to identify the policy version supporting each saved historical qualification decision.

7.12. When the editable Backtest qualification policy changes, Kanasu shall preserve earlier decisions and identify affected results as PENDING REEVALUATION for future progression. Reevaluation shall occur on Check WFA Eligibility for the selected Study, not automatically across all Studies.

7.13. Check WFA Eligibility shall check saved Backtest results against the current policy, reuse valid decisions when their evidence, policy version, and authority are unchanged, and display only currently eligible exact saved results for checkbox selection. WFA shall execute only after the user selects results and clicks Start WFA.

7.14. If required Backtest qualification settings are missing, saved results remain browsable but WFA preparation is blocked with SETTINGS REQUIRED and a clear action to configure Research Settings; no default threshold shall be invented.

7.15. If a genuine new Backtest qualification decision replaces the authority that admitted an older WFA result, the old WFA result remains historical and does not authorize a new Paper start. Kanasu shall explain NEW WFA REQUIRED rather than silently rerun WFA; repeating an unchanged eligibility check shall not create an unnecessary replacement decision.

7.16. Check Paper Eligibility shall display only exact saved WFA results supported by the current authoritative WFA PASS decision, intact current Backtest lineage, and required evidence. Start / Continue Paper Trading shall recheck that authority and the operational admission conditions before execution.

7.17. An applicable qualification check shall record or reuse a valid authoritative decision and its criteria version, distinguish rejected from insufficient, invalid, or operationally unavailable evidence, and require all currently mandatory criteria to be satisfied.

7.18. The editable net-profitable-trade-rate threshold shall be a finite value from 0% to 100% inclusive. Qualification shall use the existing accurate count-based calculation rather than rounded display metrics.
