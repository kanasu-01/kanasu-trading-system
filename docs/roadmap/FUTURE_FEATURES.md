# Kanasu — Future Features

Ideas and capabilities that may be developed after the core trading
system is stable.

---

## Market Intelligence

- Support and resistance intelligence
- Market structure detection
- Trend detection
- Breakout detection
- Volatility regime detection
- Market regime classification
- Momentum quality analysis
- Volume analysis
- Institutional activity analysis
- Big-money flow analysis

---

## News Intelligence

- Company-specific news collection
- Company news summarization
- News sentiment
- News impact classification
- Positive/negative/neutral/uncertain classification
- Event importance scoring
- Earnings/event detection
- Sector-level news analysis

---

## Social / Alternative Data

- Telegram analysis
- Social-media sentiment
- Trend detection
- Crowd sentiment
- Unusual activity detection
- Narrative detection

Important:
Data-source legality, terms of service, reliability and timestamp
integrity must be evaluated before implementation.

---

## Historical Probability Engine

Given a current market condition:

- Search historically similar conditions
- Measure subsequent price behaviour
- Calculate probability distributions
- Measure expected return
- Measure adverse excursion
- Measure favourable excursion
- Compare different market regimes

---

## Advanced Trading Intelligence

- Trade-quality score
- Signal confidence
- Dynamic position sizing
- Regime-aware strategies
- Strategy selection
- Adaptive stop placement
- Risk-adjusted opportunity ranking
- Portfolio-level opportunity ranking

---

## Advanced AI

- ML-based feature models
- NLP-based news analysis
- Embedding-based similarity search
- Time-series models
- Ensemble models
- Probability calibration
- Online/adaptive models
- Explainable AI signals

These should be developed only after the underlying data,
execution and backtesting infrastructure is trustworthy.
